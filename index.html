Architecture Overview

The Role of Solana in EduChain

Why decentralized ledgers make sense for educational credentials and what this MVP delivers today.

Connected to Solana Devnet
1. Non-Custodial & Privacy Preserving

We do NOT store transcripts, diplomas, or personal identification on-chain. Only cryptographic hashes (SHA-256 digests) and institutional authorizations are anchored.

2. Low Latency & Minimal Fees

Solana provides sub-second finality and negligible transaction costs, making high-volume credential issuance economically viable for academic institutions.

3. Transparent Demo vs. Devnet

You can use EduChain 100% without a crypto wallet. When connected to Devnet, real signatures link to the public Solana Explorer for independent auditability.

Settings & Privacy

LocalStorage Privacy Notice

EduChain stores your saved scholarships, application status choices, and personal notes directly inside your current web browser via localStorage.

  • No personal data or notes are sent to external web servers.
  • Your tracker data is not automatically synchronized across separate browsers or devices.
  • Do not store sensitive financial details, passwords, or seed phrases in notes.
